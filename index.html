FAQ

Good to know


Can Diagnosure diagnose me?

No. It gives general health information and cannot examine you. A qualified clinician must assess symptoms and make a diagnosis.

Does it search health sources?

When current health information is useful, Diagnosure can search public web sources and show links. Results can be incomplete; private or paywalled databases may not be available.

What if this is an emergency?

Call your local emergency number or go to the nearest emergency department now for symptoms such as chest pain, severe bleeding, trouble breathing, or stroke signs.

What happens to my questions?

Your question is sent to Anthropic to generate an answer. This page does not save chat history, but avoid sharing sensitive personal information.
